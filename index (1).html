    Sample events only. Dates, organisers and venues are placeholders, not confirmed BAC events.

      Offering help respectfully

      • Ask first. Say "Would you like help?" and wait for the answer.
      • Accept "no". Declining is a decision, not rudeness.
      • Speak to the person, not to a companion or interpreter.
      • Don't assume. Many disabilities are invisible and needs differ from person to person.
      • Don't touch a wheelchair, cane or assistance animal without permission.
      • Focus on access and participation. Skip the "inspiring" talk.
